🍋 Lemon Math Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

Introduction

Lemon Math ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Key Point: Lemon Math stores your data locally on your device. We do not operate servers that store your personal information.

Information We Collect

  • Images: Photos of math problems you scan are sent to Google's Gemini AI for analysis. These images are processed in real-time and are not stored on any servers we operate.
  • Usage Data: We collect anonymous usage statistics locally on your device to track your progress and improve the app experience.
  • API Key: If you provide your own Gemini API key, it is stored securely in your device's local storage.
  • Problem History: Solved problems and their solutions are stored locally on your device for your convenience.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered math problem solving services
  • To display your problem history and favorites
  • To track your achievements and learning progress
  • To provide step-by-step explanations and alternative methods
  • To improve app functionality based on anonymous usage patterns

Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Gemini AI to analyze and solve math problems. When you scan a problem:

  • Your image is sent to Google's servers for AI processing
  • Google's privacy policy applies to this data transmission
  • We recommend reviewing Google's Gemini API Terms
Note: If you use the free tier of Gemini API, Google may use your data to improve their products. With paid tier, this is disabled.

Data Storage

  • All problem history is stored locally on your device
  • Your API key is stored in the app's secure local storage
  • We do not maintain external servers for storing user data
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored data permanently

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • API keys are stored using iOS secure storage mechanisms
  • All network communications use HTTPS encryption
  • No sensitive data is transmitted to our servers (we don't have any)

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Delete: Clear your problem history at any time in Settings
  • Remove API Key: Delete your stored API key at any time
  • Offline Mode: Use offline mode to prevent any data transmission
  • Export: Export your problem history from the app
  • Contact: Request information about your data by contacting us

Children's Privacy

Lemon Math is designed to be educational and safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. The app contains no inappropriate content, advertisements, or social features.

International Users

If you are accessing the App from outside the United States, please be aware that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries where Google's servers are located.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new policy in the app with an updated date
  • Displaying a notice in the app about the changes
